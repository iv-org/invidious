Fable Vs Astra Debate Is Over

Theo - t3.gg · 41K views

Theater mode is the default and expands the same video above the related rail. Below 900 px, related videos follow the main content. The visible seek track has its own row at every width, with theater beside fullscreen. Clip start/end markers remain functional but are visually hidden so the blue progress rail is the only position signal.

Player actions use existing Video.js icons and accessible names; time and speed remain text. With focus inside the player or on Play, J/L seek ten seconds, K and Space tap toggle playback, and Space or mouse holds temporarily play at 2× before restoring the prior rate and paused state. Enhancements remain optional.
